# Candidate record
Every non-trivial candidate records:
- id and parent version/hash;
- target component and exact file/section;
- failure class, source evidence, frequency and uncertainty;
- diagnosis, hypothesis and falsifier;
- exact patch and declared changed files;
- expected gain and behaviours that must remain unchanged;
- plausible regressions, evaluation gaming and drift;
- frozen task IDs, independent evaluator, metrics, acceptance threshold, budgets;
- rollback parent; ordinary or meta edit.

Diversity means different mechanisms: a deterministic verifier, a routing rule, or a decomposition change. Synonyms for "be careful" do not constitute distinct candidates.
Keep rejected hypotheses in state. A changed validator may be a candidate for future use, but never the judge of its own promotion.
